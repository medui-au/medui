**Patient Search** finds a patient in Best Practice and opens their summary.

## Searching {#searching}

Type the surname, then optionally a comma and the first name: **`Smith`** or
**`Smith, Jo`**. Results appear as you type.

- The **surname** matches from its start: `Smi` finds Smith and Smithers.
- The **first name** after the comma matches anywhere in the first or middle name: `Smith, an`
  finds Anne and Joanna.
- Up to **50** patients are shown, by surname then first name. If the one you want isn't
  there, type more of the name.
- Inactive and deceased patients are included; their **Status** is shown on the right.

Click a patient to open their summary. Nothing in the search changes the patient's record:
MedUI only reads from Best Practice.

## If nothing is found {#nothing-found}

- Check the spelling, and try the surname alone.
- Hyphenated and double surnames: start with the first part as Best Practice has it.
- "Could not connect to patient database" means MedUI can't reach Best Practice just now:
  try again shortly, and tell the practice manager if it continues.
