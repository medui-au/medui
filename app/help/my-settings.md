**My settings** (your name at the top right) holds your own preferences. For what your login
lets you use, see [logging in and access](help:logging-in.access).

## Your preferences {#overview}

- **Your name in MedUI:** the name MedUI shows for you, if it isn't the one in Best Practice
  (a shorter or usual name, for example). It's used everywhere in MedUI: the top bar, the day
  sheet and appointments, the inbox and results, practitioner lists, and the notes the AI
  reads, so drafts use it too. Leave it blank to use your Best Practice name. Best Practice
  itself isn't changed. Redaction catches both names before anything goes to a cloud AI model.
- **Theme**, and your **email signature** for emails from Comms.
- **Patient record:** two results badges for the Disease Profile, each worded as its rule, off
  until you tick them and shown only to you (see
  [the Disease Profile](help:patient-record.disease-profile)). The practice can't set or lock
  them.
- For doctors: whether the AI's instructions start **expanded**, **Review every request before
  sending to cloud AI** (see [the Sending tab](help:ai-drafts.sending)), and the models and
  workflow you last chose for the chatbots and scribe, which MedUI remembers for next time.

A setting marked **Set by your practice** has been fixed by the practice manager and can't be
changed here. The practice also sets the starting values for new users (Application Setup →
User defaults).

## Your details card {#details-card}

Point at your name at the top right, or tab to it, to see your details as Best Practice holds
them. Click your name to open My settings.

- **Qualifications, AHPRA registration, HPI-I, provider numbers** (one per site, with the site's
  name) and **prescriber number**: from your Best Practice user record.
- **First patient visit, patient visits and patients seen:** counted from your visits in Best
  Practice. Every visit type counts (surgery, telephone, telehealth, home and so on) except
  "Non visit". Patients seen counts each patient once.
- Lines with nothing recorded are left out, so staff without these details see only their name.
- The card shows when it was read. It's read again after an hour.

MedUI only shows these details. To correct one, change it in Best Practice. Only you see your
own card.

## My usage {#usage}

**User Data → My Usage** shows what you have used MedUI for, under your own name. Only you see
it, and it never shows anyone else's use.

- **Two tabs.** *AI* has your AI runs, the share run on local models, the cost cloud providers
  reported, how many failed, and your scribe sessions. *Non-AI* has your tasks, charts opened,
  messages sent, exports to Best Practice and sign-ins.
- **Practice:** under each of your figures is the whole practice's total for the same period,
  yours included. It is a total only: it is never broken down by person or role, and your
  filters don't narrow it.
- **Looking inside a figure:** click a figure to see it broken down (by feature, model, task and
  so on). Click a row to look inside it; it becomes a filter under *Showing only*, removed with
  its ×. **Show the records** lists your entries, newest first, 50 to a page.
- Choose the **period** at the top.

The record holds no patient details, only whether a task was about a patient. If tasks from
before live recording began were imported, those taken from the web server's log have no
person, so they aren't counted here.
