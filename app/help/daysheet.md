**Appointments & Daysheet** shows one day's appointments from Best Practice, beside what has
been billed that day.

## Reading the day sheet {#reading}

- **Appointments** (left): each booking with its time, doctor, patient, reason, eligibility,
  length and who booked it.
- **Daysheet / Attendances** (right): what has been billed, with item, fee and status.
- **Date:** pick another day, or **Today** to come back. Today's day sheet says **Live** and
  refreshes itself every 2 minutes; other days don't.
- "Today" is the practice's date, in the practice's timezone (set in Application Setup).

## Colours {#colours}

Each appointment is tinted by its status in Best Practice:

| Colour | Status |
|---|---|
| Purple | Booked |
| Amber | Arrived, waiting or with the doctor |
| Green | Finished |
| Red | Did not attend |
| Grey | Unavailable (time blocked off) |

Your own appointments are outlined.

## Filtering by doctor {#filter}

The initials after **Filter:** are the doctors with appointments that day. Click initials to
show only their rows; click more to add them, or click again to take them off; **All** shows
everyone. If you have
appointments yourself, the day sheet starts on yours. Your choice is remembered on this
computer.
