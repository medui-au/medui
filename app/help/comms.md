**Comms** sends an email or SMS to a patient, or files a document in their Best Practice
record. It opens from a patient, or from the Library, an AI reply or the general chatbot, which
hand it their text.

## Choosing the patient and channel {#overview}

When Comms opens without a patient (from the Library or the general chatbot), search for the
patient first (see [finding a patient](help:patient-search.searching)). The header then shows
the patient, with the email and mobile number Best Practice holds. Choose a tab:

- **Email** or **SMS** to send to the patient;
- **Best Practice** to file a document in their record without sending anything.

Check the patient and the text before you send or file: nothing goes until you press the
button. Messages sent to the patient are listed on the right.

## Email {#email}

- **To** is filled from Best Practice; check it. **Subject**, and a **Template** if the
  practice has made some.
- Write in plain text with simple formatting (`**bold**`, lists); **Rendered** shows how it
  will look. Your signature is added if you have one.
- **Tidy Up** is an AI pass over the wording: check it after. **BP →** also files a copy in
  the patient's record.
- **Send Email** sends it.

## SMS {#sms}

The number is filled from Best Practice; check it. Up to 305 characters. **Send SMS** sends it.

## Filing to Best Practice {#file-to-bp}

The **Best Practice** tab files the text in this patient's record without sending anything to
the patient. Give it a **Title** (its name in Best Practice), check the text, then **File to
BP**. It arrives through Best Practice's import of incoming results, as a report would, and
is filed for this patient.
