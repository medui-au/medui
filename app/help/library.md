The **Library** (User Data → Library) holds the practice's general documents: information
sheets, letter templates, forms, notes. Documents aren't linked to any patient.

## Finding a document {#finding}

- Search by title, content or keywords.
- The filters show **All**, those shared with **All Users**, your **Private** ones, those for
  **Doctors**, ones you have **Hidden** from your own list, and **Deleted** ones.

## Writing and saving {#editing}

- Give the document a **title** and, if you like, **keywords** to find it by.
- **Visibility:** *All users*, *Doctors only*, or *Private* (only you).
- Type or paste into the editor. **Save** keeps it; **Save As** keeps a copy under a new title
  (save first).
- **Open** shows it as a web page; **Print**, **Copy**; **Mono** switches to a fixed-width font
  for aligned tables.

## Writing with AI {#ai}

Doctors and administrators can describe the document they want in **AI Prompt**, or ask for
changes to the current one, and the Library's AI workflow writes it. **↩ Undo** puts back the
text from before the last AI change. What it writes is a draft to check and edit (see
[AI drafts](help:ai-drafts.drafts)).

## Not linked to a patient {#patients}

**Don't type or paste a patient's name or details into a Library document.** The Library has
no patient, so the AI workflow can't remove that patient's identifiers before a cloud AI
sees the text. For anything about a patient, use the AI assistant or the patient chatbot.

To give a finished document to a patient, use **BP →** or **Email**: they open **Comms**, where
you choose the patient, check the text, and file or send it (see [Comms](help:comms.overview)).
