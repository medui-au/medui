**Governance** keeps the practice's records of complaints and adverse events, from receipt to
close, with notes, actions and reports.

## Complaints {#complaints}

Anyone can record a complaint. It moves through **received → acknowledged → under review →
resolved → closed** (or **withdrawn**), with a category, notes, actions and any disclosure
recorded along the way. A record made in error is **voided**, not deleted.

## Adverse events {#adverse-events}

Anyone can report an event or near miss. Each has a **severity** (SAC 1 catastrophic to SAC 5
near miss) and moves through **reported → triaged → under review → root-cause analysis →
action plan → monitoring → closed** (or **no action required**).

## Who sees what {#access}

- Everyone can record, and see the records they made.
- Doctors, and anyone given governance access, see and update all records, and use the
  **register** and its report.
- Assigning a record to someone needs a higher governance level.
- Practice managers can give a person governance access without changing their Best Practice
  group (**Governance Permission Overrides**), and can see voided records.

## The register {#register}

**All Governance Items** lists complaints and adverse events together, with a report for the
practice's quality and accreditation records.

## AI-use policy {#ai-policy}

**Governance → AI-use Policy** is the practice's policy on using AI in MedUI, for everyone to
read. It starts as a template; an administrator adapts it with **Edit** (in Markdown) and has it
approved. Its **Appendix A** lists the AI providers and models enabled at the moment it is opened,
printed or downloaded: whether each runs on the practice's computers or in the cloud, and what
each model is used for. **Print or save as PDF** and **Download (.md)** keep a dated copy, for the
practice's document management, whenever the policy is approved or a provider or model changes.
