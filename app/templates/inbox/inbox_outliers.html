How this list is made

The most recent inbox result for each patient and test, when it is outside its reference range:

  • Lab flag — the laboratory's own flag, as sent.
  • Above / below range — the value against the range the laboratory reported, where it sent no flag.

Results are not graded or ranked. Previous lists earlier results for the same test, as reported.
