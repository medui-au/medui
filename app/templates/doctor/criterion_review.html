Criterion review{{ help('criterion-review.overview') }}

A record-coding audit: patients whose results meet a published criterion where the condition isn't coded in the record, for a doctor to review. Each row shows the results, their dates and the criterion's source. A criterion met is not a diagnosis: whether the patient has the condition, and recording it in Best Practice, is the doctor's decision. Active patients seen in the last 24 months; the condition codes are the Disease Profile's (Admin → Mapping Tables → condition_codes, influences).
