Adds Admin → Privacy Review: a list of the AI requests in which MedUI's pre-send privacy check found something, or that a user reported, so an administrator can look at each one and record what it was. Switching it off hides the list and the review form; reviews already saved are kept. The check itself always runs, whether this is on or off.
