{# ── Staff Privacy ───────────────────────────────────────────────────── #}
Staff Privacy

When enabled, staff members' own patient records are hidden from all colleagues. Only the matched staff member can view their own record. Matching uses the USERID field in BPSDB against registered users.

Hide staff patient records from colleagues
{# ── Privacy Review (ADR-120) ────────────────────────────────────────── #}
Privacy Review{{ help('privacy-review.switch') }}

Adds Admin → Privacy Review: a list of the AI requests in which MedUI's pre-send privacy check found something, or that a user reported, so an administrator can look at each one and record what it was. Switching it off hides the list and the review form; reviews already saved are kept. The check itself always runs, whether this is on or off.

Show the Privacy Review page to administrators
