AI scope{{ help('setup.ai-scope') }}

A short scope instruction is placed in front of the system prompt of every AI call made by a workflow, keeping the AI to organising, summarising and drafting from what was said or recorded. Which wording applies: the workflow's prompt step if it sets one, otherwise the system prompt's (Prompt Manager), otherwise the practice default below. A reply the AI flags as out of scope is shown under a warning and recorded on the run.
